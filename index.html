Your private vault, one calculator away.

Hush keeps your notes, contacts, diary, photos, files and passwords in one encrypted place on your iPhone. It looks and works like a calculator until you enter your secret code.

SupportPrivacy PolicyTerms of Use

Hidden in plain sight

The app opens as a working calculator. Type your opening password and the vault appears, no extra button needed.

Encrypted on your device

Everything is encrypted with a key derived from your password. There are no accounts and no servers: your data never leaves your iPhone unless you export it.

A deletion password

A second password destroys the vault instantly and shows an empty one, for situations when you are forced to open the app.

What's inside

  • Notes with folders, colors, rich text, checklists, photos, audio and files.
  • Diary with photos, videos, audio and formatted text; import from other diary apps and export to readable pages.
  • Gallery with albums and a full-screen viewer with zoom.
  • Files with folders, import and export, and a built-in audio player.
  • Contacts in groups and Passwords in categories.
  • Encrypted backups of every section, protected by a separate password.

Hush Pro

Hush is used with a yearly Hush Pro subscription. Eligible new subscribers start with a 14-day free trial. The price is shown in the App Store in your local currency. Details are in the Terms of Use.

Hush cannot recover a forgotten opening password. Keep it somewhere safe, and create backups.
