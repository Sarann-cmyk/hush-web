Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Short version: Hush does not collect your data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it runs no servers of its own. What you store stays encrypted on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store or share any personal information. The developer has no way to see your notes, contacts, diary entries, photos, files or passwords.

Where your data lives

Everything you add to Hush is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, encrypted with AES-GCM. The encryption key is derived from your opening password and is never stored in readable form. Because of this, we cannot recover your data or your password if you forget it.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Camera — only when you choose to take a photo or video inside a note or diary entry.
  • Microphone — only when you choose to record audio.
  • Photos and Contacts — the app uses the system pickers, so it receives only the items you select. A picked contact is copied into your encrypted vault; later changes in your Contacts app are not synced.
  • Files — only files you choose to import, or locations you choose to export or back up to.

Data obtained through these permissions is used only for the action you requested and stays on your device.

Purchases

Hush Pro is an auto-renewable subscription processed by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple ID. The app reads your subscription status from Apple's StoreKit on your device to unlock the app.

Backups, sharing and export

Backups are files you create yourself. They are encrypted with a password you choose and are saved where you decide (for example, the Files app or iCloud Drive). Export, share and print actions send data only to the destination you pick in the system share sheet. We do not receive any of it.

Network use

Hush works without an internet connection, with one exception: the currency converter downloads public exchange rates when you open it: from the National Bank of Ukraine (bank.gov.ua) if the app language is Ukrainian, or from the European Central Bank (ecb.europa.eu) if it is English. The request is a plain download that contains none of your data, no account and no device identifier. Like any internet request, it reveals your IP address to that bank's server, and the bank's own privacy terms apply. The last downloaded rates are kept on your device so the converter keeps working offline.

Third parties

Hush contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. The third parties involved are Apple (App Store and system services), which handles purchases and operates the system features you use, and the National Bank of Ukraine or the European Central Bank, which publish the exchange rates described above. Apple's own privacy policy applies to Apple's services.

Tracking

Hush does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Because nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove your data from your device, delete the vault (entering the deletion password erases it immediately) or delete the app. Backups you created remain wherever you saved them until you delete those files.

Children

Hush is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
