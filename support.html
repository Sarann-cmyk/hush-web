Frequently asked questions

I forgot my opening password. Can you recover it?

No. The key that encrypts your data is derived from the password and is never stored, so neither we nor anyone else can recover the password or the data. If you have a backup, create a new vault and restore it with the backup's password.

What is the deletion password?

It is a second password you set with the opening password. Typing it on the calculator instantly and permanently destroys the vault and shows an empty one. It is meant for situations when you are forced to open the app. To see the passwords you typed while changing them, go to Settings → Change passwords: after you enter your current password, the new fields become visible.

How do I open the vault?

Hush opens as a calculator. Type your opening password on the keypad. The vault opens by itself, without pressing =. Create the vault and set the passwords first in the calculator's Settings.

How do I back up and restore my data?

Open the vault, then Settings → Data & Backup → Create Backup. Choose the sections to include and a backup password, then save or share the file. To restore, choose Restore from Backup, pick the file and enter its password. Existing items are never overwritten or deleted.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

In the app: Settings → Hush Pro → Manage Subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Hush. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

I subscribed but the vault is still locked

Open Hush Pro and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that bought the subscription and that you have an internet connection.

What happens to my data if my subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted. The vault is locked and your data stays encrypted on your device. It becomes accessible again as soon as you subscribe.

Where is my data stored? Is it synced anywhere?

Only on your device, encrypted. Hush has no accounts or servers and does not sync through any service. Use encrypted backups to move data to another device.
